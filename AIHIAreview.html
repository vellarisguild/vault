Chapter 3: The Correct Customer Journey - How It's Supposed to Work

You have over a hundred tools, but no clear path for a customer to walk. This is the Customer Journey you **MUST** create. It's the application of Hormozi's principles to the assets you already possess.

  1. 1

    THE HOOK: From Stranger to Interested

    A customer sees "The Gauntlet" ad that hits their pain point ("Working hard but not getting rich") and offers a solution ("Let AI work for you"). They click not because they want a tool, but because they want "hope."

  2. 2

    THE OFFER (THE TRAP): Risk Reversal

    They arrive at the landing page and see an irresistible offer: "7-day free trial in exchange for a credit card." This is the Risk Reversal principle from `$100M Offers`. We are telling them, "Our product is so good, we're confident enough to let you try it before you pay."

  3. 3

    THE FIRST WIN: Immediate Value Proof

    Immediately upon signing up, they are guided by the "Genesis Quest" to use the easiest, most impactful tool first (e.g., Orb of Clarity). Within 24 hours, they get their first "win" (e.g., a 1-hour meeting summarized in 5 minutes). This is the immediate value delivery that Hormozi emphasizes.

  4. 4

    THE REVELATION: From Tool to System

    After their first "win," the customer starts to realize... Vellaris isn't just a "tool," it's a "system" that will change their entire workflow. They begin to see how the other 99+ tools can solve other problems in their life. This is where Tong's "100 AI Tools Suite" idea begins to shine.

  5. 5

    THE CONVERSION: From Trial User to Member

    At the end of 7 days, overwhelmed with the value they've received, paying 2,990 THB is no longer an "expense." It becomes an "investment" that would be stupid not to make. This is the ultimate goal of a Grand Slam Offer: to make people feel stupid saying no.
