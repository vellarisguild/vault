VA Daily Workflow

  • Log in to tools and check notifications.
  • Review assigned tasks; prioritize by deadline.
  • Clarify with clients or leads as needed.
  • Track time for each task (15-min increments).

  • Submit end-of-day report summarizing completed work.

  • Review VA progress and resolve bottlenecks.
  • Confirm task updates in the tool.

Problem Escalation

VA Issues

  1. VA informs Team Lead immediately (overload, unclear instructions, technical issues).
  2. Team Lead evaluates and reallocates as needed.
  3. Escalate unresolved issues to Account Manager or Client Success Manager.

Client Issues

  1. Acknowledge within 1 hour of complaint.
  2. Investigate root cause with assigned VA.
  3. Propose solution (revised timeline, added support).
  4. Follow up within 24 hours to ensure satisfaction.
